# Fabrikam Manufacturing Customer Brief

## Customer

Fabrikam Manufacturing is a fictional industrial equipment manufacturer with
multiple plants and a distributed field-service workforce.

## Audience

Operations, plant leadership, engineering, and customer-service leaders.

## Business challenge

Plant teams spend too much time finding maintenance history, operating
procedures, and approved troubleshooting guidance. Unplanned downtime and
inconsistent handoffs make it harder to support production and customers.

## Priorities

1. Help frontline teams find approved maintenance and troubleshooting guidance.
2. Improve handoffs between plant operations, engineering, and field service.
3. Use operational data to identify issues earlier and support more consistent decisions.

## Proposed capabilities

- Connected service and work-order management
- AI-assisted knowledge retrieval and case summaries
- Governed conversational experiences for frontline teams
- Unified customer, service, and operational insights

## Expected outcomes

- Faster access to approved procedures and equipment context
- More consistent service and maintenance handoffs
- Better visibility into recurring issues and service demand
- A stronger foundation for data-informed operational decisions

## Call to action

Define a focused proof of concept for one plant, service process, or equipment
scenario.

## Visual direction

- Primary color: industrial blue
- Accent color: safety orange
- Tone: practical, precise, and operational
