# Contoso Bank Customer Brief

## Customer

Contoso Bank is a fictional regional bank serving consumers and small businesses.

## Audience

Customer service and digital banking leaders.

## Business challenge

Customers experience long wait times and inconsistent service across phone,
chat, and online banking channels.

## Priorities

1. Reduce customer-service wait times.
2. Give service agents faster access to trusted information.
3. Provide consistent and personalized support across channels.

## Proposed capabilities

- AI-assisted customer service
- Automated case summaries
- A trusted knowledge assistant
- Customer-service analytics

## Expected outcomes

- 25% reduction in average handling time
- Faster onboarding for service agents
- Improved customer satisfaction
- More consistent responses

## Call to action

Run a four-week proof of concept for the customer-service team.

## Visual direction

- Primary color: deep blue
- Accent color: teal
- Tone: trustworthy, modern, and professional
