# Adatum Retail Customer Demo Brief

> This brief uses fictional information for workshop testing. It contains no
> confidential customer data or externally approved performance claims.

## Customer

**Customer name:** Adatum Retail

**Industry:** Retail

**Customer description:**  
Adatum Retail is a fictional regional retailer offering apparel, home goods,
and seasonal products through physical stores and an e-commerce site.

**Information status:**  
Fictional and approved for workshop use.

## Audience

**Primary audience:**  
Retail operations, customer experience, digital commerce, customer service,
marketing, and technology leaders.

**Conversation objective:**  
Explore how Adatum Retail could create a more connected shopping experience
while helping store associates and service representatives respond with
relevant information and consistent guidance.

## Business challenge

Customers may encounter disconnected experiences as they move between the
website, contact center, and physical stores. Store associates and service
representatives may need to search across multiple systems to understand an
order, locate approved product information, or determine the appropriate next
step.

This conversation will explore how Adatum Retail could connect these moments
while maintaining human judgment, customer choice, and appropriate controls
over customer and operational data.

## Priorities

1. **Create continuity across shopping channels**  
   Explore how customers could move between digital, service, and in-store
   experiences without unnecessarily repeating information or restarting their
   journey.

2. **Help employees find trusted information faster**  
   Discuss how associates and service representatives could access approved
   product, order, policy, and customer information in the flow of work.

3. **Deliver relevant experiences with appropriate governance**  
   Clarify how permitted customer signals could support more useful engagement
   while respecting consent, security, privacy, and data-retention policies.

## Future-state journey

### Current challenge

**Title:** Customer context can become fragmented across channels

**Description:**  
A customer begins shopping online, contacts customer service about product
availability, and later visits a store. Each interaction may rely on different
information, requiring the customer or employee to reconstruct the journey.

### Improved employee or operational experience

**Title:** Relevant context and approved guidance in the flow of work

**Description:**  
A store associate or service representative can review available order and
interaction context, locate approved product and policy information, and
determine the next action. Suggested information remains subject to employee
review and established business rules.

### Resulting customer or business experience

**Title:** A more coherent retail conversation

**Description:**  
The customer can receive clearer and more consistent assistance across
supported channels. Adatum Retail can evaluate whether this approach improves
the experience before considering broader adoption.

## Proof-of-concept discussion

**Indicative duration:** Four weeks

**Scenario and scope:**  
Validate one connected customer-assistance scenario beginning with an online
order or product inquiry and continuing through either customer service or a
selected store. Confirm the participating channel, required data, approved
knowledge sources, employee roles, escalation path, and integration boundaries
before implementation.

**Proposed participants:**  
Include a retail operations lead, store associate, customer-service
representative, digital commerce owner, customer-experience lead, technology
owner, data owner, and representatives from security, privacy, compliance, and
accessibility.

**Validation measures:**  

- Whether employees can find relevant approved information
- Whether guidance is understandable and consistent
- Whether employees retain appropriate review and decision authority
- Whether the scenario respects role-based access and data policies
- Whether customers can move between the selected channels with clearer continuity
- Whether the workflow is practical for participating store and service teams

These measures are evaluation criteria, not promised results.

**Next action:**  
Schedule a planning workshop with the account team and Adatum Retail to confirm
the scenario, participating store or team, owners, data requirements, approved
evidence, success criteria, and review checkpoints.
